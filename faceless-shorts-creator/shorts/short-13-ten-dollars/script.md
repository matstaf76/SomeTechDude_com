# The $10 Decision — kids money short (ages 5+)

**Starring:** Jake & Maya (age 8, cousins) · **Lesson:** knowing what's a trap changes everything.

**Sub-type:** faceless image-story. Supplied character art + backgrounds, animated in Remotion
(ken-burns / parallax), word-synced captions, ElevenLabs v3 emotion-tagged VO.

- Format: 1080×1920 @30, **~143s** across 17 beats (one per script page).
- Pace: ~2.25 words/sec + 0.7s breath — gentle storyteller, not shorts-fast.
- Emotional engine: **worry → naming → relief**. The turn is beat 13, where the threats get named.
- **No CTA outro** (locked brand rule): end on the lesson; beat 17 reframes beat 1 for a seamless loop.

## ⚠️ Length

At 143s this is ~3× the repo's 42s shorts. Three options, your call:

1. **Ship it long** — YouTube Shorts allows up to 3 min. Keeps all 17 pages as written.
2. **Condense to ~55s** — this is a rewrite, not a trim. The script is 294 VO words;
   55s buys ~97 words across 17 beats, or ~108 if consolidated to 10.
   So roughly **two-thirds of the words have to go**. Dropping the 2 beats marked ✂
   below is only the start — every remaining line needs shortening too.
3. **Split in two** — beats 1–9 as *the trip*, 10–17 as *the traps*. Two ~70s shorts, one series.
   Keeps every word, and the traps land in their own episode.

Recommendation: **3**. The naming beats (11–14) are the payload and deserve room;
halving the words to hit 55s would cost exactly the part the lesson rests on.

## Art checklist

Characters are drawn once and reused across beats — these are the character sheets:

- **jake** — Jake, 8, cousin — needs: neutral, excited, worried, grinning
- **maya** — Maya, 8, cousin — needs: neutral, counting money, worried, grinning
- **iron-banker** — Kind banker, gray beard, deep purple + gold armor — needs: waving, kneeling, clapping, winking
- **jack-ruiner** — Jack the Ruiner — hides behind high prices; menacing but not scary for age 5
- **the-swipe** — The Swipe — not a person: a shimmer/crackle effect that flatters whatever it touches
- **grandma** — Grandma — single warm appearance, handing over gift cards

Backgrounds, one per location:

- **home-morning** — Doorstep / kitchen, warm morning light
- **school-bus** — Yellow bus exterior and interior window seat
- **fairweather** — The city of money — glass towers, coin motifs, wide establishing
- **bank-hall** — Bank interior, museum-like: gold, glass, tall ceilings
- **toy-shop** — Shop window packed with toys and candy
- **vault** — Giant vault door, coins stacked high
- **food-counter** — Cafeteria counter, menu board with prices, candy rack
- **lunch-table** — Long table, trays, whole class seated

Drop them in `media/projects/short-13-ten-dollars/` and the composition reads them
via `staticFile('projects/short-13-ten-dollars/<name>.png')`.
